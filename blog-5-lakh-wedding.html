₹5 Lakh Mein Shaadi Kaise Karein — Complete Roadmap

📅 2026 ⏱️ 9 min read ✍️ Smart Shaadi AI ← Saare Articles

₹5 lakh mein shaadi? Possible hai — agar aap smart planning karo. Is guide mein exact roadmap hai 100-150 guests ke saath dignified, memorable wedding ke liye ₹4.5-5.5 lakh budget mein.

💡 Key Rule: ₹5L wedding mein guest list control sabse important decision hai. 100 guests at ₹500/plate = ₹50,000 catering only. 200 guests = ₹1 lakh+ — already 20% budget gone on catering alone.

✦ Real Couple Story

Neha & Vikram — Bhopal, March 2025

Neha aur Vikram dono government employees hain. Budget tha ₹5 lakh, guests the 120. Logon ne kaha "impossible" — lekin unhone kiya. Kaise? Community hall ₹25,000 mein book kiya. Caterer se bulk deal kiya — ₹350/plate veg buffet. Photography mein local talented photographer dhundha jo ₹25,000 mein full day coverage deta tha. Decoration khud family ne ki. Total kharch: ₹4.8 lakh. "Sabse badi learning thi ki 'expensive' aur 'memorable' alag cheezein hain," Vikram kehte hain.

Venue Options Under ₹1 Lakh

Community halls (panchayat bhawan, municipal halls): ₹5,000-30,000 per day. Temple premises (puja hall): often free for small ceremonies. Government cultural centers: ₹10,000-40,000 per day. Relative's large home/terrace: potentially free. Church/gurdwara halls: community members often have subsidized access.

Catering Strategy

Local caterer (not branded) for 100 people: ₹600-800 per plate veg = ₹60,000-80,000 including service. Menu: 4-5 simple but fresh dishes rather than 12 mediocre ones. Homemade sweets from family members instead of catered dessert counter — saves ₹15,000-25,000 and adds personal touch.

Photography on Budget

Find a good photography student or fresh professional — quality can be excellent at 50-60% lower price. Alternatively: hire a photographer for ceremony only (2-3 hours) instead of full day — save ₹15,000-20,000. Key moments: pheras, varmala, family photos. Rest can be captured on phones by family.

₹5 lakh wedding is absolutely doable with the right priorities. Remember: memorable weddings are about warmth and family — not expensive props.
