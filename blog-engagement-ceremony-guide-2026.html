Engagement Venue — Options aur Costs

Home Engagement (₹0-30K venue cost)

Ghar pe engagement sabse intimate aur cost-effective option hai — especially 30-60 guests ke liye. Decoration pe focus karo, venue cost zero. Garden ya terrace pe beautiful outdoor setup ho sakta hai ₹20-40K decoration mein.

Farmhouse / Resort (₹40-1.5L)

Best value option for 80-150 guests. Natural setting, photos beautiful, outside catering allowed usually. Pune ke Lonavala, Delhi ke Chattarpur/Gurgaon, Bangalore ke Yelahanka — all excellent farmhouse options.

Hotel Banquet (₹1-5L)

Premium experience, AC, in-house catering, professional setup. 5-star mein ₹3-8L. Mid-range hotels ₹80K-2L — good for families who want hassle-free event.

Rooftop / Terrace Venue (₹30-80K)

Trending in 2026 — unique ambiance, photogenic, evening events especially beautiful. Many restaurants aur cafes offer private rooftop booking.

Engagement Decoration Trends 2026

2026 ki engagement decoration trends jo photos mein beautiful lagte hain:

  • Floral arch + balloon installation: ₹15K-50K — Instagram must-have
  • Neon sign "He Asked, She Said Yes": ₹3K-8K rental — trending element
  • Fairy light canopy: ₹8K-20K — magical evening ambiance
  • Fresh flower ring backdrop: ₹20K-60K — gorgeous for couple photos
  • Minimalist greenery: ₹10K-25K — modern, elegant, less is more

Engagement Catering Guide

Engagement catering typically lighter hoti hai than wedding — cocktail/high-tea format popular hai:

  • Cocktail/snacks format: ₹250-500/person — perfect for 2-3 hour evening engagement
  • Full dinner: ₹600-1,200/person — for more formal engagements
  • Lunch/brunch engagement: ₹400-800/person — daytime events, lighter feel
  • Home-catered: Family cooked ₹100-200/person — most intimate option

Tip: Engagement pe elaborate buffet zaroori nahi — pass-around snacks + sit-down dessert table bahut photogenic aur guests ko pasand aati hai.

  2. 2 mahine pehle: Photographer book, catering confirm, decoration decide
  3. 1 mahina pehle: Outfits buy/order, invitations send (digital free!)
  4. 2 hafte pehle: Rings finalize, RSVP count confirm
  5. 3 din pehle: Decoration setup confirm, catering headcount give
  6. Day of: Setup 2-3 ghante pehle, photographer briefing, family coordination

Engagement Rings — Budget Guide

Engagement ceremony ke rings (ceremony rings, not necessarily the final engagement ring):

  • Simple gold bands: ₹5,000-15,000 per pair — traditional, timeless
  • Diamond/precious stone ring: ₹20,000-2L+ — depends on carat and quality
  • Lab-grown diamonds: ₹15,000-80,000 — same look, 60-70% cheaper than natural
  • Silver rings for ceremony: ₹2,000-8,000 — practical if upgrading later

Important: Ceremony ke liye expensive rings zaroori nahi — meaningful matter karta hai, price nahi.

Engagement Photography — Shot List

Engagement ceremony ke must-have shots:

  • Ring exchange close-up (hands)
  • Couple first look after ring exchange
  • Both families together
  • Couple with parents (both sides)
  • Candid celebration moments
  • Detail shots — decoration, cake, rings on table
  • Group photos — friends separately
