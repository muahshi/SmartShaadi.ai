Wedding Insurance India 2026 — Kyun Zaroori Hai aur Kya Cover Hota Hai

📅 2026-07-05 ⏱️ 11 min read ✍️ SmartShaadi AI Team

Shaadi ki planning karte waqt, hum bahut saari cheezein sochte hain, lekin ek cheez jo aksar bhool jaate hain woh hai wedding insurance. Yeh ek aisi cheez hai jo aapki shaadi ko surakshit kar sakti hai, lekin iske bare mein bahut kam log jaante hain. Indian couples ko shaadi ki planning karte waqt bahut saari mushkilein aati hain, jaise ki vendor cancellation, mausam ki samasya, aur medical emergency. In sabhi mushkilon se bachne ke liye, wedding insurance ek zaroori cheez hai.

Wedding insurance ek aisi policy hai jo aapki shaadi ko surakshit karti hai, agar kisi bhi tarah ki samasya aati hai. Ismein aapko vendor cancellation, weather, medical emergency, aur anya samasyaon ke liye cover milta hai. Yeh policy aapko shaadi ki planning karte waqt bahut saari mushkilein se bachaati hai, aur aapko apni shaadi ki suraksha ke liye ek shanti milti hai.

Wedding insurance ki zaroorat ko samajhne ke liye, humein yeh samajhna hoga ki shaadi ki planning karte waqt kya-kya mushkilein aati hain. Shaadi ki planning karte waqt, humein vendor ko book karna hota hai, catering ke liye arrangement karna hota hai, aur anya samasyaon ka samna karna hota hai. Agar kisi bhi tarah ki samasya aati hai, toh wedding insurance aapki madad karti hai.
