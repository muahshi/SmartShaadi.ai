Pune Wedding Cost 2026 — Lonavala se Koregaon Park Tak Complete Guide

📅 2026 ⏱️ 11 min read ✍️ Smart Shaadi AI ← Saare Articles

Pune — Maharashtra ka yeh city ab ek major wedding destination ban raha hai. Mumbai ke paas hone ki wajah se yahan destination feel milti hai Mumbai ki prices ke bina. Is guide mein Pune aur Lonavala ke real 2026 wedding costs hain.

💡 Pune vs Mumbai: Same quality ke venue Pune mein Mumbai se 30-40% saste milte hain. Aur Lonavala choose karo toh resort feel + countryside 25-35% premium pe.

Lonavala Wedding — Complete Cost Breakdown

Lonavala sabse popular destination hai Pune couples ke liye — aur Mumbai couples ke liye bhi. 2-day weekend wedding estimate: Resort (60 rooms, hall): ₹3-6L. Catering (2 days, 150 guests): ₹3.5-5L. Decoration: ₹1.5-2.5L. Photography: ₹1-1.5L. Transport (Mumbai/Pune pickup): ₹50K-1L. Total: ₹10-16L for intimate destination feel.

💡 Lonavala Secret: Week ki shuruat mein (Monday-Wednesday) book karo — resorts 35-40% discount dete hain. Same resort, same experience, 40% sasta!
