Indian couples ko shaadi ki planning karte waqt kai chunautiyon ka saamna karna padta hai, aur winter wedding ki planning to aur bhi challenging ho jaati hai. October se February tak, yeh season India mein shaadiyon ke liye sabse popular hota hai, lekin saath hi yeh sabse mehnga bhi hota hai. Fog photography, heating costs, aur outdoor arrangements jaise factors couple ko confuse kar dete hain. Lekin agar aapko sahi tips aur strategies mil jaayein, to aap apni winter wedding ko bhi memorable aur affordable bana sakte hain.

Shaadi ki planning karte waqt, couples ko apne budget ko manage karna padta hai, aur yeh decision lena padta hai ki kahaan pe kitna paisa kharch karna hai. Winter wedding ki planning mein, heating costs, venue decorations, aur catering jaise factors ko consider karna padta hai. Agar aap Delhi mein shaadi ki planning kar rahe hain, to aapko yeh jaankar khushi hogi ki yeh city winter wedding ke liye perfect hai, lekin saath hi yeh bhi jaan lena zaroori hai ki kaise aap apne budget ko optimize kar sakte hain.

Is article mein, hum aapko winter wedding ki planning ke liye complete guide denge, jismein aapko best season, best budget, aur best tips milenge. Winter Wedding India 2026 — Quick Stats aur Overview

📊 2026 Key Numbers

India mein winter wedding season (October-February) mein lagbhag 60% shaadiyan hoti hain. Is season mein, average wedding budget ₹25-30 lakhs hota hai. Delhi, Mumbai, aur Bangalore jaise cities mein winter wedding ki demand sabse zyada hoti hai, jiske kaaran yeh cities mein wedding venues aur services ki prices bhi sabse zyada hoti hain. Winter wedding ki planning karte waqt, couples ko average 6-8 mahine pahle booking shuru karni padti hai, taaki unhein apne choice ke hisaab se venue aur vendors mil sakein.

Heating costs bhi night wedding mein zyada hoti hain, kyunki aapko outdoor heating arrangements karne padte hain.

Best Winter Wedding Cities

India mein kai cities hain jahan winter wedding ki demand sabse zyada hoti hai. Delhi, Mumbai, aur Bangalore jaise cities mein winter wedding ki prices sabse zyada hoti hain, lekin yeh cities mein aapko best venues aur services bhi milengi.

Delhi mein winter wedding ki planning karte waqt, aapko ₹20-30 lakhs ka budget rakhna padega, jabki Mumbai mein ₹25-40 lakhs ka budget rakhna padega. Bangalore mein ₹15-25 lakhs ka budget rakhna padega.

Early Booking Discount Strategy

Winter wedding ki planning karte waqt, couples ko early booking discount strategy ka use karna chahiye. Agar aap 6-8 mahine pahle booking karte hain, to aapko venues aur vendors ki prices kam milengi.

Aapko yeh bhi dhyan rakhna chahiye ki kuch vendors early booking discount offer karte hain, lekin kuch vendors ki prices fixed hoti hain. Aapko apne budget ko optimize karne ke liye vendors ki prices compare karni chahiye.

Kahan Galti Karte Hain Log — 5 Common Mistakes

Winter wedding ki planning karte waqt, couples kai common mistakes karte hain jo unke budget ko affect karte hain.

  • Venue ki prices ko kam aaneka, jisse budget exceed ho jaata hai.
  • Catering ki prices ko kam aaneka, jisse budget exceed ho jaata hai.
  • Decorations ki prices ko kam aaneka, jisse budget exceed ho jaata hai.
  • Photography ki prices ko kam aaneka, jisse budget exceed ho jaata hai.
  • Heating costs ko ignore karna, jisse budget exceed ho jaata hai.
