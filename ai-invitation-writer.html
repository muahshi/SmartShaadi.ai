💡 Perfect Invitation Ke 5 Tips

  • ✍️
    Dono families ka naam zaroori hai — Indian tradition mein parents ke naam include karna bahut important hai, especially formal invitations mein.
  • 📅
    Date aur time clearly likho — "15 February 2026, Ravivar" jaisi full date se confusion nahi hoti.
  • 🌐
    Wedding website se guests excited hote hain — Tab 4 mein free mini website milti hai jisme countdown, venue map aur RSVP hota hai.
  • 🖼️
    AI image se WhatsApp invitation alag dikhti hai — Text ke saath ek beautiful image attach karo — log screenshot lete hain!
  • 🔄
    Multiple styles try karo — Ek hi details se Poetic, Traditional aur Romantic — teen alag invitations try karo. Sab free hain!
