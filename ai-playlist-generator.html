🎵 DJ Ko Brief Karne Ke 5 Golden Tips

  • 📋
    List 2-3 din pehle bhejo — Day-of list kaam nahi aati. DJ ko songs dhundhne ka time chahiye. Must-play aur avoid clearly mark karo.
  • ⚡
    Energy flow plan karo — Mehendi = medium, Sangeet = build to peak, Baraat = max energy, Reception = start medium build to high. Abrupt energy jumps crowd ko disturb karte hain.
  • 🔊
    Volume transitions important hain — Pheras mein volume low karo — sacred moments. DJ ko pehle se timing guide do: "8:30 PM pheras start, volume down."
  • 😢
    Vidai ke liye separate DJ/speaker arrange karo — Main DJ stage pe ho sakta hai — Vidai ke paas ek small speaker aur playlist ready rakho.
  • 📱
    Backup playlist ready rakho — DJ ke phone mein bhi playlist download karwa lo. Technical issue ho toh Spotify/YouTube se chal sake.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawaal

2026 top sangeet songs: Kala Chashma, Gallan Goodiyaan, Morni Banke, Naacho Naacho, Desi Beat, Nagada Sang Dhol, Ainvayi Ainvayi. Latest 2024-26 hits: Tere Vaaste, Rasiya, Teri Baaton Mein. AI se generate karo — exact songs aur artist names milenge.
Vidai best songs: Babul Ki Duaein (Classic), Mora Piya, Alvida (Rock On), Tujhe Bhula Diya, Kanchi Re, Woh Lamhe, Bidaai Ho Bidaai. Ek tip: Akele Hain (unplugged version) bahut emotional hai — DJ ko pehle se batao.
Best practice: (1) Ceremony-wise list banao — Mehendi, Sangeet, Baraat alag alag, (2) Must-play ⭐ mark karo, (3) Timing guide likhao — 8:30 PM Pheras, (4) 3 din pehle WhatsApp karo, (5) Backup songs bhi bhejo. Yahan se DJ Note copy karo — sab included hai.
Pheras ke time: Soft instrumental ya devotional music best. Suggestions: Teri Mitti (instrumental), Raghupati Raghav (soft version), Satrangi Re (unplugged), Tum Hi Ho (soft). Volume low rakhein — sab sun sake. DJ ko pehle batao "Pheras = volume 30% se zyada nahi."
