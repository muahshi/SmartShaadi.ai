💡 Wedding Planning Ke 6 Golden Tips

  • 💒
    Venue sabse pehle book karo — Popular venues 9-12 mahine pehle book ho jaate hain. Yeh sabse important aur time-sensitive booking hai. Pehle venue, phir sab kuch!
  • 📸
    Photographer 2nd priority — Achhe photographers bhi 8-10 mahine pehle fully booked hote hain. Portfolio dekho, reviews check karo, phir book karo.
  • 📋
    Guest list pehle finalize karo — Venue size, catering cost, invitation count — sab guest list pe depend karta hai. Isko jaldi confirm karo.
  • 🌸
    Decor aur caterer 4-5 mahine pehle — Inhe extra time milega best work dene ke liye. Multiple vendors se quotes lo, phir decide karo.
  • 👗
    Bridal outfit 4-6 mahine pehle — Custom outfits mein 3-5 mahine lagte hain. Fitting sessions ke liye time chahiye. Der na karo.
  • 💰
    10-15% contingency hamesha rakho — Unexpected kharche hamesha aate hain. Buffer ke bina stress aata hai. Smart planning = buffer planning.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawaal

Ideal timing 9-12 mahine pehle hai. Venue aur photographer sabse pehle fully book hote hain isliye jaldi shuru karna zaroori hai. 6 mahine mein bhi acchi planning possible hai lekin kuch choices limited ho jaati hain. 3 mahine se kam time mein "rush planning" mode mein jaana padta hai.
Priority order: (1) Date aur Venue confirm karo, (2) Photographer book karo, (3) Guest list finalize karo, (4) Caterer decide karo, (5) Decor theme choose karo. Yeh 5 kaam baaki sab ke foundation hain.
Destination wedding ke liye minimum 12 mahine planning chahiye. Travel logistics, hotel bookings guests ke liye, local vendors dhundhna, legal permissions — sab extra time lete hain. Udaipur, Goa jaisi popular destinations ki venue availability bhi 18+ mahine pehle se limited hoti hai.
