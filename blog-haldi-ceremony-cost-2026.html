Haldi Decoration — DIY vs Professional

Haldi decoration mein sabse bada scope hai budget save karne ka — kyunki yeh mostly flowers aur props hote hain jo DIY ho sakte hain:

DIY Haldi Decoration (₹15,000-35,000)

  • Marigold flower strings: Phool mandi se wholesale ₹50-80/meter
  • Yellow dupatta backdrop: ₹800-1,500 mein gorgeous wall banta hai
  • Terracotta pots + marigold: ₹200-400/pot, 10-15 pots kaafi hain
  • Turmeric powder bowls: Traditional + photogenic — almost free
  • String lights + yellow balloons: ₹2,000-4,000 mein magical atmosphere

Professional Haldi Decoration (₹50,000-2 lakh)

  • Elaborate flower arch + canopy
  • Custom printed backdrop
  • Prop stations (ring toss, photo booth)
  • Coordinated color scheme throughout venue
  • Setup + breakdown service included

Honest advice: Haldi ke liye mid-range decoration sufficient hai — ceremony ki rawness aur authenticity hi photography mein best lagti hai. Over-decorated Haldi kabhi kabhi artificial lagti hai.

Haldi Photography — Kitna Budget Rakhein

Haldi ke photos wedding ke baad sabse zyada share hote hain social media pe — isliye photography invest worth it hai. Lekin ₹35,000 se zyada Haldi pe photography mein lagana usually unnecessary hai:

  • Budget (₹8-15K): 3-4 ghante coverage, 150-200 edited photos — good enough for most
  • Mid-range (₹18-30K): Full day coverage, 300+ photos, some candid — recommended
  • Premium (₹35-80K): Film-style editing, drone, reels — only if important to you

Haldi Ceremony — Ghar Pe vs Venue Pe

Ghar pe Haldi sabse authentic aur cost-effective hoti hai — family involvement automatic hoti hai, logistics simple hoti hain:

  • Ghar pe Haldi: Venue cost ₹0, decoration space limited but manageable, catering easy — total ₹40-80K mein excellent Haldi
  • Outdoor/venue Haldi: More guests possible, elaborate decoration, ₹80K-2.5L range — good for large families or Instagram-worthy setup
  • Farmhouse Haldi: Outdoor natural setting, ₹20-60K venue, photos beautiful — excellent mid-range option

Haldi Catering — Kya Serve Karein

Haldi casual event hai — full meal zaroori nahi. Smart approach:

  • Snacks + drinks: Samosa, pakora, chai, nimbu paani — ₹80-150 per person, perfect for 50-100 people
  • Lunch/brunch: Agar afternoon Haldi hai, proper meal serve karein — ₹300-600 per person
  • Sweets + traditional items: Ladoo, barfi — ₹50-100 per person, always appreciated
  • Home-cooked: Family cooked food is most authentic and cheapest — lakhs bachao, memories banao

Haldi Planning Timeline

Haldi typically wedding se 1-2 din pehle hoti hai. Planning timeline:

  • 2-3 mahine pehle: Photographer book karo (demand zyada hai Haldi ke liye)
  • 1 mahina pehle: Decoration decide karo (DIY ya professional)
  • 2 hafte pehle: Outfit finalize, catering confirm
  • 3 din pehle: Phool order karo (fresh chahiye)
  • 1 din pehle: Decoration setup, props arrange
