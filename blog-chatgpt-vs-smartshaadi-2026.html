Kahan Galti Karte Hain Log — 5 Common Mistakes

Wedding planning mein, aapko kayi mistakes ho sakti hain, jo aapki wedding ko spoil kar sakti hain. Aaiye 5 common mistakes dekhte hain, jo aapko avoid karni chahiye.

  • VENUE KI BOOKING MEIN DELAY: Agar aap venue ki booking mein delay karte hain, to aapko ₹10,000 se ₹20,000 tak extra pay karna pad sakta hai.
  • CATERING KI PLANNING MEIN KAMZORI: Agar aap catering ki planning mein kamzori karte hain, to aapko ₹5,000 se ₹10,000 tak extra pay karna pad sakta hai.
  • DECORATION KI PLANNING MEIN KAMZORI: Agar aap decoration ki planning mein kamzori karte hain, to aapko ₹3,000 se ₹6,000 tak extra pay karna pad sakta hai.
  • ENTERTAINMENT KI PLANNING MEIN KAMZORI: Agar aap entertainment ki planning mein kamzori karte hain, to aapko ₹2,000 se ₹4,000 tak extra pay karna pad sakta hai.
  • BUDGET KI PLANNING MEIN KAMZORI: Agar aap budget ki planning mein kamzori karte hain, to aapko ₹10,000 se ₹20,000 tak extra pay karna pad sakta hai.
