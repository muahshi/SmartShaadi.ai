LED Band — 2026 Ka Popular Choice

LED bands mein performers LED costumes pehnte hain aur instruments bhi LED lights se decorated hote hain — night baraat mein spectacular dikhta hai. Cost 30-40% zyada hoti hai regular brass band se lekin photos aur videos mein wow factor bahut zyada hai. Agar baraat raat mein ho toh LED band best investment hai.

Budget Baraat Strategy 2026

Budget (under ₹25K): 1 dhol + basic DJ car — perfectly festive aur enough for most baraats. Mid-range (₹30-60K): Brass band + ghodi + basic DJ — complete traditional experience. Premium (₹70K+): LED band + DJ truck + ghodi + fireworks — grand statement.

Ghodi Tips

Ghodi rent: ₹8,000-30,000 per event (city-wise vary). Includes handler, decoration, saddle. Booking 1-2 months advance required in wedding season. Always confirm: kya ghodi well-trained hai crowded baraat ke liye? Handler experienced hai? Insurance covered hai agar accident ho?

Booking Tips — Avoid Surprises

  • 2-3 months advance book karo Oct-Feb season mein
  • Contract mein exact member count likho — kuch bands kam log bhejte hain
  • Overtime charges pehle clarify karo — ₹3,000-8,000/hour extra hota hai
  • Payment: 50% advance, 50% on day — kabhi 100% advance mat do
  • Fireworks ke liye local permissions check karo pehle
