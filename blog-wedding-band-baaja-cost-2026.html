Traditional Band — Kya Include Hota Hai

Ek complete wedding band package mein yeh hota hai:

  • Instruments: Brass instruments (trumpet, trombone, tuba), dhol, nagada, shehnai
  • Uniformed members: Band wale uniform mein aate hain — sherwani/kurta
  • Lighting: Most bands fluorescent tube lights ya LED carry karte hain (raat ke liye)
  • Duration: Typically 2-4 ghante, phir overtime
  • Repertoire: Bollywood hits, folk songs, classical wedding songs — confirm karein advance mein

Band Hire Karte Waqt Kya Check Karein

  • Live performance video maango: Recent wedding ka real video — edited promo nahi
  • Members count confirm karo: Quote mein 15 members likhein, actual mein 10 na aayein
  • Song list share karo: SmartShaadi AI Playlist Generator se baraat ki specific songs list do
  • Overtime rate fix karo: Per hour overtime ₹3,000-8,000 — contract mein likhaao
  • Lighting confirm karo: Raat ki baraat mein lighting zaroor chahiye — included hai ya extra?
  • Transport arrangement: Band members ki transport aapki responsibility hai ya unki?

Band Baaja Negotiate Kaise Karein

Band wale negotiate karne pe expect karte hain — yeh standard practice hai:

  1. 3 quotes lo: Compare karein — same members count, same duration
  2. Off-season advantage: May-September mein 15-20% discount milta hai
  3. Advance payment offer: "Aaj advance denge" — ₹3,000-5,000 discount possible
  4. Bundle deal: Band + dhol ek hi party se = 10-15% cheaper
  5. Specific songs maango: Song list de ke show karo aap serious client ho

Dhol Only — Budget Ka Best Option

2-3 professional dhol players baraat ke liye kaafi hain agar band ka budget nahi hai. Dhol:

  • ₹5,000-15,000 mein 2-3 ghante
  • Energy aur enthusiasm guaranteed
  • Modern Bollywood + traditional beats dono
  • Phir bhi very traditional feel
  • DJ se combine karo — best combination

Hidden Costs — Band Booking Mein

  • Overtime charges: Most expensive hidden cost — ₹3-8K per hour after agreed time
  • Transport: Outstation band ke liye travel + accommodation — ₹2K-8K extra
  • Tip/nazrana: Cultural expectation — ₹2,000-5,000 for good performance
  • Generator for DJ: If using DJ for baraat, dedicated power needed

❓ Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Mini band (8-10 members): ₹15-35K. Full band (15-20 members): ₹25-80K depending on city. Delhi/Mumbai mein 30-40% zyada hai smaller cities se. Dhol only: ₹5-15K — budget option.
Traditional families: Band must hai — emotional aur traditional value high. Modern couples: DJ more versatile (Bollywood songs exact version). Best combination: Baraat entry mein band/dhol, phir venue pe DJ — dono ka maza milta hai reasonable budget mein.
Short baraat (30 min walk): 8-10 members enough. Long baraat (1+ hour): 15-20 members better — energy maintain hoti hai. Jitne bade venue mein jaana ho utne zyada members chahiye sound coverage ke liye. Cost per member: ₹1,500-3,000 approximately.
