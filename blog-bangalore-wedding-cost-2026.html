Bangalore Wedding Cost 2026 — Tech City Mein Shaadi Ka Asli Kharcha

📅 2026 ⏱️ 12 min read ✍️ Smart Shaadi AI ← Saare Articles

Bangalore — India ki tech capital — mein shaadi plan karna IT couples ke liye ek unique challenge hai. Long working hours, out-of-town guests, international budgets — sab kuch alag hota hai Bangalore weddings mein. Is guide mein 2026 ke real numbers hain.

💡 Bangalore Wedding Average 2026: 200-250 guests, 2 days — ₹12-25 lakh. Mumbai se 25-30% sasta lekin prices 2024 se 18% badh gayi hain.

Bangalore Catering 2026 Per Plate Rates

Bangalore South Indian + North Indian fusion catering sabse common hai. 2026 rates:

  • South Indian traditional: ₹600-900 per plate (satvik, budget-friendly)
  • North Indian + South Indian fusion: ₹900-1,400 per plate
    • Multi-cuisine premium:₹1,400-2,200 per plate
  • 5-star hotel level: ₹2,200-4,000 per plate

Coorg Destination Wedding — Worth It?

Coorg se Bangalore sirf 5-6 hours hai. Coffee plantation resorts ₹3-10 lakh per weekend mein available hain. 50-80 guest intimate wedding ke liye yeh excellent value hai — per person cost regular Bangalore venue se comparable hoti hai lekin experience 10x premium lagta hai. Orangi Estate, Rainforest Retreat, Coorg Wilderness Resort — yeh popular choices hain.

IT Couples Ke Liye Special Tips

Bangalore ke IT couples ki unique challenge: bahut colleagues invitations expect karte hain. Smart approach: ek separate office-colleagues cocktail reception rakho (simpler, less expensive) aur family-friends main wedding alag. Yeh 30-40% guests manage karne ka smart way hai. Long weekends (3-day holidays) mein Bangalore wedding karein taaki out-of-station guests bhi aasani se aa sakein.

💡 Bangalore 2026 Tip: March-April mein Bangalore mein wedding karna best hai — achi weather, off-peak prices (15-20% discount), aur no traffic disruptions from festival season.

Bangalore Mein Wedding Planning Tips

  • Traffic management: Weekend weddings mein guests ko reach karna mushkil — venue ka location carefully choose karein
  • South Indian catering: Authentic South Indian thali ₹600-900/plate — budget-friendly aur authentic
  • Coorg option: 4-5 ghante drive pe destination wedding feel ₹18-28L mein milti hai
  • Off-season advantage: May-August mein Bangalore mein 15-20% savings milti hai
