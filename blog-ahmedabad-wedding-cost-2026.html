Budget Overview

Ahmedabad mein Gujarati shaadi ka budget kayi cheezon par nirbhar karta hai, jaise ki venue, catering, decoration, photography, aur anya vyavasthaayein. Yadi aap ek basic Gujarati shaadi ki yojana bana rahe hain, to aapko lagbhag 5 lakh se 10 lakh rupaye kharch karna pad sakta hai. Lekin, yadi aap ek high-end shaadi chahte hain, to yeh kharch 20 lakh se 50 lakh rupaye tak ja sakta hai.

Yahaan kuch mahatvapoorna kharche diye gaye hain jo aapko apni shaadi ki yojana banate samay dhyaan mein rakhne chahiye:

  • Venue: 1 lakh se 5 lakh rupaye
  • Catering: 1.5 lakh se 3 lakh rupaye
  • Decoration: 50,000 se 1 lakh rupaye
  • Photography: 20,000 se 50,000 rupaye
  • Anyay vyavasthaayein: 50,000 se 1 lakh rupaye
