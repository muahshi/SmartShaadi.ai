Ideal: 9-12 mahine pehle. Minimum: 6 mahine. Rush job: 3-4 mahine (possible but stressful). Venue aur caterer — yeh do cheezein sabse pehle book karein. Baki sab baad mein ho sakti hain.
Common misses: Marriage registration (legal), changing room at venue, parking arrangement, backup photographer, honeymoon bookings, thank-you cards, wedding night accommodation, vendor tip budget.
Pre-events booking: 4-6 mahine pehle. Mehendi artist: Very popular ones 6 mahine+ advance. Caterer for pre-events: 2-3 mahine. Decoration for mehendi: 1-2 mahine. SmartShaadi Timeline Generator automatically pre-event deadlines include karta hai.
Start backward from wedding ceremony time. Typical flow: Makeup (4-5 hrs before), Getting ready (2 hrs), Photography (1 hr pre-ceremony), Ceremony, Cocktail hour, Reception dinner. Share timeline with ALL vendors — photographer, caterer, decorator sab.
Typical: 25-30% advance on booking, 50% 1 month before, balance on wedding day. Keep all receipts. SmartShaadi Dashboard se vendor payments track kar sakte hain — kab kitna dena hai sab visible rahega.
