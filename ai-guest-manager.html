💡 Guest Management Ke 6 Golden Tips

  • 💒
    Guest list venue capacity se pehle banao — Kitne log aayenge yeh decide karo, phir venue book karo. Reverse mat karo — sabse common mistake hai yeh.
  • 📱
    Personalized invitation = zyada response — "Priya ji, aapko specially bulaya hai..." wali invitation ko 3x zyada confirm milte hain generic message se.
  • ⏳
    3 RSVP reminders send karo — Pehla 4 hafte pehle, doosra 2 hafte pehle, teesra 1 hafte pehle. WhatsApp read receipts dekho.
  • 🍽️
    Caterer ko 5% extra order karo — Indian weddings mein 10-15% no-show hota hai but last-minute additions bhi aate hain. 5% buffer safe hai.
  • 👑
    VIP guests pehle confirm karo — Bade log decline karein toh seating plan aur functions adjust karne ka time mile. Inhe 6-8 hafte pehle invite karo.
  • 💾
    Guest data yahan save rahega — Yeh tool browser mein automatically save karta hai. Koi account nahi chahiye. Data aapke device pe safe hai.
