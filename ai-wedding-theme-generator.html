🎨 Wedding Theme Choose Karne Ke 6 Golden Rules

  • 🌸
    Lehenga pehle chunein, theme baad mein — Pehle bridal lehenga finalize karo — phir uska color palette theme decide karega. Clash automatically avoid ho jaata hai.
  • 💡
    Lighting sabse bada impact deta hai — ₹50K ke flowers + ₹15K ki lighting se behtar hai ₹20K ke flowers + ₹45K ki premium lighting. Warm fairy lights ne average venues ko magical banaya hai.
  • 📅
    Season ke saath theme match karo — Summer mein dark maroon mein guests uncomfortable. Winter mein pastels washed out. Season + theme + venue — teen cheezein ek saath sochni chahiye.
  • 🌼
    Local flowers 5x saste hote hain — Genda phool, roses, tuberose, mogra — Indian flowers sabse impactful aur 80% saste. Seasonal flowers double attractive lagte hain.
  • 📸
    Photography ke liye theme consider karo — White backdrop + dark lehenga = stunning. AI-generated color palette photographer ko share karo — woh accordingly lighting set karenge.
  • 👗
    Family color coordination theme ka hissa hai — Groom ki family ka color, bride ki family ka color plan karo. Group photos mein coordinated colors magical lagte hain.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawaal

2026 trending: (1) Modern Pastel — soft pinks, sage green, ivory, (2) Earthy Bohemian — beige, terracotta, greens, (3) Jewel Tones — emerald, sapphire, burgundy, (4) Classic Royal Mughal — timeless. Budget ke hisab se: ₹3-7L ke liye Floral Garden best value, ₹15L+ ke liye Royal Mughal sabse impactful.
Total wedding budget ka 15-20% decor pe recommended. Approximate: Flowers ₹50K-2L, Lighting ₹30K-1L, Stage backdrop ₹40K-2L, Centerpieces ₹500-2000/table. Lighting aur flowers combined sabse zyada impact — baaki mein save karein.
Decorator ko share karein: (1) Color palette with HEX codes — AI se copy karo, (2) 5-10 reference photos, (3) Must-have elements list, (4) Must-avoid list, (5) Budget breakdown per element. 3 decorators se quotes lo. HEX codes dene se "approximately" ka game band ho jaata hai.
Lighting pe invest karo pehle! Correct warm lighting mein simple decor premium lagti hai. Formula: 40% budget lighting, 40% flowers/stage, 20% centerpieces + misc. Fairy lights + warm Edison bulbs = any venue magical.
Haan! Best DIY: (1) Marigold garlands — ₹100/meter vs ₹500+ market, (2) Paper flower backdrop — ₹2000 vs ₹15000, (3) Mason jar centerpieces, (4) Ribbon + fairy light canopy. Avoid DIY for: Stage, Main entrance arch for large weddings.
