Information You Provide Directly: When you use our planning tools, chatbot, or contact forms, you may provide information such as names, wedding dates, city preferences, budget information, guest details, and contact information. We collect only what you choose to share.

Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and time spent on pages. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies.
