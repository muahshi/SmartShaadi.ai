Buffet vs Sit-Down Dinner — Kaunsa Sasta Hai?

Buffet aur sit-down mein price difference 30-40% tak ho sakta hai. Yahan honest comparison hai:

Buffet ke Fayde

Buffet mein per plate cost sit-down se kam hoti hai kyunki service staff kam chahiye. Guests ka experience bhi better hota hai — woh apni marzi se kha sakte hain. Wastage control karna aasaan hota hai aur refill ke liye extra charge nahi lagta.

Sit-Down Dinner ke Fayde

Sit-down dining mein ek royal feel aata hai jo especially destination weddings mein acchi lagti hai. Portion control hota hai jo wastage kam karta hai. Formal occasions ke liye ya small intimate gatherings ke liye yeh better option hai.

Hidden Charges Jo Contractor Nahi Batata

Yeh woh charges hain jo most caterers upfront mention nahi karte:

  • Service Charge: 10-15% extra — usually quote mein shamil nahi hota
  • GST: 5-18% depending on restaurant-style ya standalone caterer
  • Decoration Charge: Table setting, linens, floral centerpieces — ₹50-200 per table extra
  • Overtime Charge: Function 10 PM ke baad extend hone par ₹20,000-50,000 extra
  • Generator/Power Charge: Open venues mein ₹15,000-30,000 extra
  • Tasting Session: Kuch caterers charge karte hain ₹2,000-5,000

⚠️ Real Example: ₹1,000 per plate quote mila + 15% service + 18% GST = actual cost ₹1,333 per plate. 300 guests = ₹1 lakh difference!

Smart Ways To Save On Catering

Yeh 5 proven tips real couples ne share kiye hain:

  1. Live Counters Kam Karein: 3 live counters ki jagah 1-2 signature ones rakhein. Wahi popular hote hain aur 30% cost bachat hoti hai.
  2. Local Seasonal Menu: Out-of-season vegetables aur imported items avoid karein. Local produce 40-50% sasta padta hai.
  3. Dessert Strategy: Full dessert counter ki jagah 3-4 signature mithai aur ek cake — equally impressive aur 35% sasta.
  4. Welcome Drinks Simplify Karein: Welcome drink counter mein 1-2 mocktails + nimbu pani kaafi hai. 6-7 options maintain karna mehnga padta hai.
  5. Advance Booking Discount: 3-4 months pehle book karenge toh 10-15% discount milta hai most caterers se.

Questions Jo Caterer Se Poochhein

Contract sign karne se pehle yeh zaroori sawaal poochho: Kya quote mein GST aur service charge shamil hai? Extra hours ka charge kya hai? Staff kitna aayega? Backup plan kya hai agar koi item available na ho? Tasting session free mein milega ya charge hoga? Last-minute cancellation policy kya hai?
