Day vs Night — Kya Fark Padta Hai?

Surat mein shaadi ki planning ke liye, aapko yeh decide karna padta hai ki aapko day wedding karni hai ya night wedding. Day wedding ke liye, aapko ₹5 lakh se ₹10 lakh tak pay karna padta hai, aur night wedding ke liye, aapko ₹10 lakh se ₹20 lakh tak pay karna padta hai. Day wedding ke liye, aapko venues, catering, decoration, aur outfits par ₹3 lakh se ₹5 lakh tak pay karna padta hai, aur night wedding ke liye, aapko ₹5 lakh se ₹10 lakh tak pay karna padta hai.
