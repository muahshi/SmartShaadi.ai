Sangeet Ceremony Cost 2026 — Planning, Decoration aur Complete Guide

📅 2026-04-05 ⏱️ 11 min read ✍️ SmartShaadi AI Team
Advertisement

Shaadi ki planning karne mein sabse badi problem jo Indian couples face karte hain, wo hai budgeting aur expenses ko manage karna. Sangeet ceremony bhi ek aisa event hai jo ki bahut hi expensive ho sakta hai, especially agar aapne DJ, live singers, aur choreographers ko hire kiya hai. Toh, aaj hum baat karenge Sangeet ceremony cost ke baare mein aur kaise aap apne budget ko manage kar sakte hain.

Ek aam Indian shaadi mein, Sangeet ceremony ka budget approximate ₹50,000 se ₹5 lakh tak ho sakta hai, yeh aapke choices aur requirements pe depend karta hai. Lekin, agar aapne thoda planning aur research kiya, toh aap apne budget ko bahut kam kar sakte hain. Aaj hum aapko batayenge ki kaise aap Sangeet ceremony ko affordably plan kar sakte hain, bina quality compromise kiye.

Humne dekha hai ki kai couples apne Sangeet ceremony ko plan karte waqt bahut si galtiyan karte hain, jaise ki decoration, music, aur performances ke liye bahut jyada paise kharch karne. Lekin, agar aapne thoda samajhdari se kaam liya, toh aap apne budget ko control kar sakte hain aur ek memorable Sangeet ceremony ka aanand le sakte hain.

Tips and Guide — Sangeet Ceremony Planning

Ek successful Sangeet ceremony plan karne ke liye, aapko kuch tips aur guides follow karni chahiye. Sabse pehle, aapko apne budget ko define karna chahiye aur apne requirements ko prioritize karna chahiye. Phir, aapko apne vendors ko select karna chahiye aur apne decorations, music, aur performances ko plan karna chahiye.

Agar aapne apne Sangeet ceremony ko DIY (do-it-yourself) karne ka plan kiya hai, toh aapko apne decorations, music, aur performances ko apne aap plan karna chahiye. Lekin, agar aapne apne Sangeet ceremony ko professional vendors ke saath plan kiya hai, toh aapko unke saath coordinate karna chahiye aur apne requirements ko unhein communicate karna chahiye.

Kahan Galti Karte Hain Log — 5 Common Mistakes

Ek Sangeet ceremony plan karte waqt, log kai galtiyan karte hain. Yeh 5 common mistakes hain jo log karte hain:

  • Apne budget ko define nahi karna
  • Apne requirements ko prioritize nahi karna
  • Apne vendors ko select karne mein samay nahi dena
  • Apne decorations, music, aur performances ko plan karne mein samay nahi dena
  • Apne guests ko communicate nahi karna

Hidden Costs Jo Quote Mein Nahi Hote

Ek Sangeet ceremony plan karte waqt, kai hidden costs hote hain jo quote mein nahi hote. Yeh 6 hidden costs hain:

  • Decoration ke liye extra materials ka cost: ₹5,000 - ₹10,000
  • Music ke liye extra equipment ka cost: ₹3,000 - ₹6,000
  • Performances ke liye extra rehearsals ka cost: ₹2,000 - ₹4,000
  • Lighting aur sound ke liye extra equipment ka cost: ₹2,000 - ₹4,000
  • Food aur beverages ke liye extra servings ka cost: ₹5,000 - ₹10,000
  • Photography aur videography ke liye extra hours ka cost: ₹3,000 - ₹6,000
