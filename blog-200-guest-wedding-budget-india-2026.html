200 Guest Wedding Budget India 2026 — ₹8L se ₹25L Complete Guide

📅 2026 ⏱️ 10 min read ✍️ Smart Shaadi AI ← Saare Articles

200 guests — India mein yeh ek typical mid-size wedding size hai. Na too intimate, na too large. Is guide mein 2026 ke real numbers hain 200 guests ke wedding ke liye.

💡 2026 Range: 200 guests, 2-day wedding — ₹8 lakh (budget) to ₹30 lakh (premium) depending on city and choices.

✦ Real Couple Story

Priya & Arjun — Jaipur, January 2026

200 guests, Jaipur ka heritage venue, 2-day celebration — budget ₹18 lakh. Priya ki family chahti thi ₹25 lakh kharch karein, lekin Arjun ne SmartShaadi Budget Calculator se breakdown dikhaya. Discover kiya ke caterer ki per-plate rate ₹180 zyada thi market se. Negotiation ke baad ₹36,000 bachaye sirf catering mein. Phir mid-week wedding rakhi — venue ₹60,000 sasta mila. Total saving: ₹4.2 lakh — woh paisa honeymoon pe kharch kiya! "Numbers dekhne ke baad family bhi maan gayi," Arjun kehte hain.

How To Save ₹1.5L On a ₹15L Budget

1. Move to weekday (+₹50K saving on venue). 2. Trim guest list to 175 (-25 guests = ₹60,000 saving). 3. Negotiate photography combo (save ₹15,000). 4. Use digital + 75 physical invitations (save ₹20,000). Total savings: ₹1.45 lakh with no visible quality reduction.

❓ Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

2026 mein 200 guests ke liye catering cost: Veg buffet ₹800-1,200/plate = ₹1.6-2.4 lakh. Non-veg ₹1,000-1,500/plate = ₹2-3 lakh. Yahan per-plate negotiate karna bahut important hai — ₹100/plate discount = ₹20,000 total saving.
City pe depend karta hai. Delhi: ₹2-5 lakh. Jaipur/Bhopal: ₹80,000-2 lakh. Mumbai: ₹3-7 lakh. Farmhouses aur community halls 5-star se 50-60% saste hote hain aur ambiance bhi achha hota hai.
SmartShaadi ka free Guest Manager tool use karein. Guest name, meal preference, RSVP status — sab track karo. Catering cost automatically calculate hoti hai. 200 guests manage karna ek click mein easy ho jaata hai.
Common hidden costs: Parking charges (₹5,000-15,000), generator backup (₹8,000-20,000), security staff (₹3,000-8,000), changing rooms (₹2,000-5,000), serving staff tip. Budget mein 10-15% buffer zaroor rakhein.
Standard ratio: 1 server per 10-15 guests = 15-20 servers. Buffet setup mein thoda kam chalega. Caterer se service staff list maango — yeh often extra charge hota hai.
