💡 Wedding Budget Ke 6 Golden Rules

  • 💒
    Venue pe 25-30% se zyada mat lagao — Yeh sabse common mistake hai. Venue ek hi din ke liye hai, photography memories zindagi bhar ke liye. Balance zaroori hai.
  • 📅
    Off-season mein 20-30% savings — April-September mein same venue 20-30% sasta milta hai. June-August mein sabse zyada discount. Smart choice!
  • 🤝
    Package deals se 10-15% bachao — Venue + catering + decor ek hi vendor se lene par often 10-15% discount milta hai. Bundle negotiate karo.
  • 📱
    Digital invitation se ₹50,000+ bachao — Printed cards pe ₹50K–₹1.5L waste hota hai. AI se banao, WhatsApp pe bhejo — aur stylish bhi hota hai!
  • 🍽️
    Local caterer = 30-40% savings — Branded caterer se 30-40% mehenga. Local experienced caterer same quality par kam price — taste bhi better hoga.
  • 💰
    10% emergency buffer hamesha rakho — Jo bhi budget banao usmein 10% last-minute emergencies ke liye. Last-minute kharche hamesha aate hain!

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawaal

Delhi NCR mein average Indian wedding (300-400 guests, 2 din, decent venue) ka kharcha ₹15L se ₹35L tak hota hai. Budget wedding ₹8-12L aur grand wedding ₹50L+ ho sakti hai. Peak season (Nov-Feb) mein 20-30% zyada kharcha hota hai.
Haan bilkul! Tier 2-3 city mein 100-150 guests ke saath ₹5 lakh mein dignified wedding possible hai. Hall rent, simple catering, basic decor, ek photographer — sab fit hota hai. Smart planning se ₹5L mein bhi memorable shaadi hoti hai.
Top 3: (1) Venue — 25-35%, (2) Catering — 20-30%, (3) Decor — 10-15%. Yeh teen milake 60-70% budget consume karti hain. Photography+Jewelry+Outfit 20-25% le sakti hain. Baaki music, transport, misc mein split hota hai.
Common hidden costs: Pandit/Maulvi fees (₹15-50K), mehendi wali (₹10-30K), makeup artist trial (₹5-15K), generator backup (₹10-25K), staff tips (₹10-20K), last-minute flowers (₹10-30K), parking, sound system upgrade. Total 10-15% extra budget zaroori hai.
Budget: ₹30-70K (local photographer), Mid: ₹70K-1.5L (experienced, full day + album), Premium: ₹1.5-4L (top photographer, drone, SDE). Overall budget ka 8-12% photography pe recommended hai. Photography pe kanjusi mat karo — memories permanent hain!
April-September (off-season) mein 20-35% savings hoti hai. June-August mein sabse zyada discount. Weekday weddings 10-20% saste hote hain. Off-season + weekday = sabse smart financial decision wedding planning mein.
