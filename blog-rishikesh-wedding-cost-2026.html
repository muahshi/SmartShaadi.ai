Rishikesh Wedding Cost 2026 — Quick Stats aur Overview

📊 2026 Key Numbers

Rishikesh mein shaadi ki average cost ₹20 lakh se ₹50 lakh tak ho sakti hai, jo ki venue, catering, aur decoration ke anusar badh sakti hai. Rishikesh mein shaadi ki season April se June aur September se November tak hoti hai, jab mausam suhaavna hota hai. Rishikesh mein shaadi ki planning ke liye, couples ko kam se kam 6-8 mahine phele se planning karni padti hai.

Day vs Night — Kya Fark Padta Hai?

Rishikesh mein shaadi ki planning karte waqt, couples ko day vs night wedding ki choice karni padti hai. Day wedding ki cost ₹15 lakh se ₹30 lakh tak ho sakti hai, jo ki venue, catering, aur decoration ke anusar badh sakti hai. Night wedding ki cost ₹20 lakh se ₹40 lakh tak ho sakti hai, jo ki venue, catering, aur decoration ke anusar badh sakti hai.

Day wedding ki advantage yeh hai ki ismein natural light ka use kiya ja sakta hai, jisse photography aur decoration mein bahut suhaavna look aata hai. Night wedding ki advantage yeh hai ki ismein artificial light ka use kiya ja sakta hai, jisse ek dramatic aur romantic look aata hai.
