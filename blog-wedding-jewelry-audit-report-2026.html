Bhopal Case Study: The Sarafa Secret

📍 Location: Chowk Sarafa vs. Bittan Market

Hamare ek client ne Bhopal ke ek premium branded showroom mein 22K gold chokar select kiya.

  • Branded Showroom: Making charges 18% (approx ₹1,100/gm). Total: ₹4,25,000.
  • Smart Shaadi Audit Recommendation: Humne wahi design ek trusted HUID-compliant boutique jeweller se karwaya Chowk Sarafa mein.
  • Result: Making charges 8% (approx ₹500/gm). Total Savings: ₹37,500!

Key Takeaway: Har baar brand tag quality ki guarantee nahi hota, HUID tag hota hai.

Day vs. Night: The Lighting Trap

FAQs: Your Jewelry Doubts Answered

1. Kya local jewellers se gold lena safe hai?

Ji bilkul, bas check karein ki jewellery par **HUID Hallmark** ho aur aapko proper GST bill mile.

2. Bhopal mein best gold kahan milta hai?

Bhopal ka **Chowk Sarafa** authentic designs aur competitive making charges ke liye best hai, jabki **New Market** premium experience ke liye hai.

3. Diamond ya Gold, 2026 mein kya lena chahiye?
