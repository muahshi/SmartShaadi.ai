Airbrush vs HD vs Traditional — Kya Difference Hai?

Traditional Makeup

Regular brushes aur sponges se apply hota hai. Budget-friendly lekin photos mein thoda heavy dikh sakta hai. For daytime functions ya smaller budget shaadis appropriate hai. 4-6 hours stay typical.

HD Makeup (High Definition)

Camera ke liye specially formulated — photos aur videos mein flawless dikhta hai. Real life mein natural feel. 2026 mein most popular choice for wedding day. 8-10 hours stay typical.

Airbrush Makeup

Compressor se fine mist spray hoti hai — extremely lightweight feel, long stay (12+ hours), sweat-proof. Groom bhi airbrush lete hain increasingly. Most expensive but best result specially in humid weather.

Hair + Makeup Package — Negotiate Kaise Karein

Artist se sirf makeup na lo — hair + makeup combo lo. Aksar 20-30% discount milta hai. Additional suggestion: bride ki sister/mother ka makeup bhi usi artist se karo — bulk discount milega. Trial session ₹2,000-8,000 extra hoti hai but mandatory hai before final booking.

⚠️ Important: Trial session skip mat karo. Agar artist trial mein confident nahi karta ya portfolio se match nahi karta — cancel karo. Cancellation policy pehle samjho.

Hidden Charges Jo Artist Nahi Batata

  • Travel charges (15+ km ke liye ₹500-2,000)
  • Early morning surcharge (5-6 AM start ke liye)
  • Extra functions (if same artist 3+ functions kare — each function alag charge)
  • Products cost agar premium international brands use karein
  • Assistant charge (celebrity artists akele nahi aate)

Makeup Trial Kyu Zaroori Hai — 5 Reasons

Bahut dulhanein trial skip karti hain "paise bachane" ke liye. Yeh soch galat hai — trial ek investment hai. Yahan 5 reasons hain kyun trial zaroor karein:

  • Skin reaction test: Kuch products se allergy ho sakti hai — trial mein pata chalta hai, wedding pe nahi.
  • Artist ki working speed jaanein: 4 ghante lagaate hain ya 6? Wedding timeline crucial hai.
  • Style match karein: Portfolio mein achha lagta hai, aap pe alag lag sakta hai — skin tone, features alag hote hain.
  • Staying power test: 6 ghante baad bhi makeup fresh hai ya nahi — trial mein check karo.
  • Communication establish karein: Artist ko exactly samjhaein kya chahiye — misunderstanding wedding pe nahi hoti.

Makeup Artist Ko Kya Poochein — 10 Must-Ask Questions

Booking se pehle yeh 10 questions zaroor poochein — inka jawab sab kuch clear kar deta hai:

  1. Konse products use karenge? (brand names)
  2. Trial session included hai ya extra?
  3. Touch-up kitne ghante tak available hoge?
  4. Family members ka makeup extra charge mein?
  5. Early morning (5-6 AM) availability hai? Extra charge?
  6. Cancellation policy kya hai?
  7. Travel charges kya honge?
  8. Airbrush machine apni laayenge?
  9. Pehle ki brides ke references de sakte hain?
  10. Final payment kab?
