2026 Ki Top Budget-Saving Tips

Hum real couples se mile feedback ke based pe yeh proven tips share kar rahe hain:

1. Off-Season Mein Book Karein

May-July aur September-October mein wedding karein. Venue rates mein 30-40% discount milta hai. Agar December mein date nikalna forced hai, toh weekday (Mon-Wed) choose karein — weekend se 15-20% sasta padta hai.

2. Guest List Trim Karein — Seriously

100 guests se 150 karna matlab sirf 50 log nahi — matlab hai ₹3-5 lakh extra kharcha (catering + seating + invitations + favors sab milake). Har unwanted guest ko budget mein ek real number assign karein — decision aasaan ho jaata hai.

3. Buffet vs Sit-Down

Sit-down dinner mein per plate cost buffet se 35-45% zyada padti hai. Sabse smart option: lunch reception + hi-tea snacks — full dinner reception se 40% sasta aur guests bhi comfortable rehte hain.

4. Local Flowers Use Karein

Imported roses aur exotics ki jagah seasonal desi flowers use karein. Marigold, champa, mogra — yeh in-season hain aur 60% sasta padta hai. Ek ₹2L decor budget mein imported flowers ki jagah local flowers se ₹80K bach sakti hai.

5. Photography Package Negotiate Karein

Photographers ko batao ki aap drone coverage nahi chahiye ya reels ka ek edit kam kar sakte hain. Typically ₹15,000-25,000 ki negotiation possible hai ek package mein.

⚠️ Budget Trap: Venues aksar "all-inclusive" packages dete hain jo expensive lagte hain. Hamesha breakdown maango aur individually compare karo. Aksar 20-25% bachaya ja sakta hai.

Conclusion: Kitna Rakhein Budget?

Honestly, 2026 mein 200 guests ke liye ₹12-20 lakh ek realistic budget hai Tier-1 city ke liye. Tier-2 cities mein ₹8-15 lakh mein bahut achhi shaadi ho sakti hai. Key mantra: venue aur catering pe invest karein, baaki categories mein smart choices karein. Ek memorable shaadi ke liye maximum spending zaruri nahi — smart spending jaruri hai.
