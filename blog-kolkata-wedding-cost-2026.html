Bengali Wedding Ki Khas Traditions — Budget Impact

Gaye Holud (Bengali Haldi)

Bengali Gaye Holud sirf Haldi nahi hoti — yeh ek full celebration hai tumeric ceremony ke saath, gifts exchange, music, aur grand feast. Typically ek din pehle hoti hai. Budget: ₹80K-3L depending on scale. Photography bhi separately karwate hain — alag cost.

Sampradaan (Main Ritual)

Bengali wedding ka main ritual Sampradaan hai — yeh Pandit ke under hota hai, specific timing pe. Experienced Bengali pandit ₹15,000-50,000+ charge karte hain. Shubho Drishti, Mala Badal, Sindoor Dana — har ritual ki specific samagri hoti hai.

Bou Bhaat (Reception)

Wedding ke baad Bou Bhaat groom ki family ki taraf se hosted hoti hai — ek alag event. Yeh kabhi kabhi wedding se zyada grand hoti hai! Budget alag se rakhna zaroori hai — ₹3-8L additional for mid-range.

Bengali Wedding Catering — Bhoj

Bengali Bhoj weddings ka highlight hota hai — fish (especially Rohu, Hilsa), mutton, sweets (mishti), aur elaborate multi-course meal. Per-plate: ₹600-1,200 (veg), ₹900-1,800 (non-veg Bengali full course). Hilsa fish inclusion: ₹200-400/plate extra during season.

Kolkata Wedding Mein Saving Tips

  • New Town vs South Kolkata: Same quality venue ₹2-4 lakh sasta milta hai New Town mein. 40-minute drive ka fark hai.
  • December-January pe pressure: Peak season mein 20-25% premium. February ya October mein book karo — savings significant hain.
  • Bengali sweets: Local mishti dokan se order karo — hotel se 60% sasti aur zyada authentic. Balaram Mullick, KC Das jaise established shops better value dete hain.
  • Single photography vendor: Wedding aur Gaye Holud dono ek photographer se book karo — 15-20% bundle discount milta hai.
  • Bou Bhaat simplify karo: Grand Bou Bhaat optional hai — intimate family gathering bhi traditional aur meaningful hoti hai.

Kolkata Wedding Photographers — Rate Guide 2026

Kolkata mein wedding photography excellent quality available hai reasonable rates pe:

  • Budget photographers: ₹25,000-45,000 — decent quality, good for tight budgets
  • Mid-range: ₹50,000-1 lakh — good candid + traditional, 2 photographers
  • Premium: ₹1-2.5 lakh — cinematic photography, drone, full team
  • Tip: Kolkata ke photographers Durga Puja season mein best portfolio banate hain — unka Puja work dekh ke hire karo, wedding quality similar hogi

Hidden Costs — Kolkata Specific

  • Generator/power: Load shedding ka risk — generator backup ₹8K-20K must hai
  • Sound system: Live music Bengali weddings mein popular hai — ₹15K-50K extra
  • Multiple events transport: 3 events mein family movement ₹10K-30K logistics cost
  • Ritual items (samagri): Bengali ceremonies mein specific items ₹5K-15K

Conclusion

Kolkata mein 2026 mein ek complete Bengali wedding — Gaye Holud, Wedding, Bou Bhaat — ₹14-25 lakh mein excellent quality ke saath ho sakti hai. Smart planning se ₹3-5 lakh bachana easily possible hai — zone selection, off-season booking, aur vendor negotiation se.
