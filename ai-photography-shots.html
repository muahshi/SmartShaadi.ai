Must-Have Wedding Shots — Reference

💄 Bridal Prep Essentials
  • Lehenga flat-lay detail shot
  • Jewelry close-up — necklace, maang tikka
  • Makeup artist applying kajal
  • Bride looking in mirror (candid)
  • Hands with mehendi + ring close-up
  • Bride + family laughing moment
💍 Ceremony Must-Shots
  • Varmala exchange — wide + close
  • Pheras — each phera separately
  • Sindoor daan — extreme close-up
  • Ring exchange — fingers close-up
  • Pandit Ji + fire — atmospheric
  • Parents watching — emotion capture
😢 Vidai Emotional Shots
  • Bride hugging mother — tears moment
  • Father's last hug before car
  • Rice throwing from car window
  • Bride waving goodbye — wide shot
  • Groom supporting bride
  • Car driving away — silhouette
🌅 Golden Hour Poses
  • Couple walking away — backlit
  • Bride spinning lehenga — wide shot
  • Forehead touch — close-up
  • Groom whispering to bride
  • Silhouette against sunset
  • Laughing candid — natural joy

📸 Photographer Se Best Kaam Kaise Nikaalein — 6 Tips

  • 📋
    Shot list 3 din pehle bhejo, din pe nahi — Photographer ko prepare karne ka time chahiye. Location scout, lighting check — day-of list useless hai.
  • 😢
    Vidai ke liye dedicated photographer rakhein — 2 photographers hain toh ek sirf Vidai ke liye assign karo. 20-minute emotional window — miss mat karo.
  • 🌅
    Golden hour ke liye 30 min calendar mein block karo — "6:00-6:30 PM — Couple Portraits Only" likh do. Reception guests ke beech ye time nikalna crucial hai.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Family list specific names ke saath do — "Family photos" nahi — "Bride ke parents + siblings, Groom ke Nana-Nani exact names." Photographer ko combinations pata hone chahiye.
  • 💍
    Detail shots ke liye props ek jagah rakhein — Jewelry, shoes, invitation card, flowers, ring box — sab ek basket mein. Photographer ko dhundhna nahi padega.
  • 🤳
    Pheras + Sindoor mein phone-free zone declare karo — Uncle Ji ka iPad sabke shots mein aa jaata hai. Announce karo pehle — warna regret hoga.
