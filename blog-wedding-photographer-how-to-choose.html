Day vs Night — Kya Fark Padta Hai?

Wedding photography ke liye, day aur night dono time ka apna apna importance hota hai. Daytime mein, aapko natural light milta hai, jo aapki photos ko aur bhi beautiful banata hai. Lekin, nighttime mein, aapko artificial light ka use karna padta hai, jo aapki photos ko aur bhi dramatic banata hai.

FactorDay WeddingNight Wedding
LightingNatural lightArtificial light
Photography StyleCandid, naturalPosed, dramatic
Cost₹20,000₹30,000
Guests100-200200-500
FoodLunch, snacksDinner, dessert
DecorationsFlowers, balloonsLighting, drapes

Agar aap day wedding chahte hain, toh aapko ₹20,000 se ₹50,000 tak kharch karna hoga. Lekin, agar aap night wedding chahte hain, toh aapko ₹30,000 se ₹1,00,000 tak kharch karna hoga. Aapko apne budget ke hisaab se decision lena hoga.
