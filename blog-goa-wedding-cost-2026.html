Day vs Night — Kya Fark Padta Hai?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko yeh decide karna hoga ki aap day wedding ya night wedding karna chahte hain. Dono mein apne-apne fayde aur nuksaan hain. Day wedding mein, aapko sunlight ki zaroorat nahi hoti, aur aapko decorations par utna kharch nahi karna padta. Lekin night wedding mein, aapko lighting aur decorations par zyada kharch karna padta hai.

North Goa vs South Goa — Kya Fark Hai?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko yeh decide karna hoga ki aap North Goa ya South Goa mein karna chahte hain. Dono mein apne-apne fayde aur nuksaan hain. North Goa mein, aapko zyada options milte hain, aur aapko Baga Beach, Calangute Beach, aur Anjuna Beach jaise famous beaches milte hain. Lekin South Goa mein, aapko zyada peace aur tranquility milta hai, aur aapko Palolem Beach, Colva Beach, aur Benaulim Beach jaise beautiful beaches milte hain.

North Goa mein, aapko ₹10 lakh se ₹20 lakh tak ka budget rakha ja sakta hai. Lekin South Goa mein, aapko ₹8 lakh se ₹15 lakh tak ka budget rakha ja sakta hai. Yeh aapke budget aur preferences par nirbhar karta hai.

Resort Wedding vs Private Beach Wedding — Kya Fark Hai?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko yeh decide karna hoga ki aap resort wedding ya private beach wedding karna chahte hain. Dono mein apne-apne fayde aur nuksaan hain. Resort wedding mein, aapko zyada amenities milte hain, aur aapko swimming pool, spa, aur gym jaise facilities milte hain. Lekin private beach wedding mein, aapko zyada intimacy aur exclusivity milta hai, aur aapko apne guests ke saath quality time bitaane ka mauka milta hai.

Resort wedding mein, aapko ₹15 lakh se ₹30 lakh tak ka budget rakha ja sakta hai. Lekin private beach wedding mein, aapko ₹10 lakh se ₹20 lakh tak ka budget rakha ja sakta hai. Yeh aapke budget aur preferences par nirbhar karta hai.

Permit Requirements — Kya Zaroori Hai?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko permit requirements ke baare mein pata hona chahiye. Aapko police permit, noise permit, aur beach permit ki zaroorat hoti hai. Aapko ₹5,000 se ₹10,000 tak ka kharch karna pad sakta hai.

Catering — Kya Options Hain?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko catering ke baare mein pata hona chahiye. Aapko seafood, vegetarian, aur non-vegetarian options milte hain. Aapko ₹2 lakh se ₹5 lakh tak ka budget rakha ja sakta hai.

Sunset Timing — Kya Mahatvapurn Hai?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko sunset timing ke baare mein pata hona chahiye. Aapko 4 pm se 7 pm ke beech mein sunset hota hai. Aapko is timing ke hisaab se apne wedding ko plan karna chahiye.

Monsoon vs Winter Season — Kya Fark Hai?

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko monsoon vs winter season ke baare mein pata hona chahiye. Monsoon season mein, aapko ₹5,000 se ₹10,000 tak ka discount mil sakta hai. Lekin winter season mein, aapko ₹10,000 se ₹20,000 tak ka extra kharch karna pad sakta hai.

Kahan Galti Karte Hain Log — 5 Common Mistakes

Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, aapko kuch common mistakes se bachna chahiye. Yeh mistakes aapke budget aur experience ko affect kar sakte hain.

  • Insufficient research: Aapko Goa mein wedding plan karne ke liye, sufficient research karna chahiye. Aapko venues, caterers, aur decorators ke baare mein pata hona chahiye.
  • Incorrect budgeting: Aapko apne budget ko accurately plan karna chahiye. Aapko venues, catering, decorations, aur photography ke baare mein pata hona chahiye.
  • Poor communication: Aapko apne vendors ke saath poor communication se bachna chahiye. Aapko unke saath clearly communicate karna chahiye.
  • Insufficient planning: Aapko apne wedding ko insufficient planning se bachna chahiye. Aapko apne wedding ke liye timeline banani chahiye.
  • Not having a backup plan: Aapko apne wedding ke liye backup plan hona chahiye. Aapko unexpected situations ke liye prepared hona chahiye.

  • Miscellaneous expenses: Aapko miscellaneous expenses ke baare mein pata hona chahiye. Yeh aapke bill mein ₹10,000 se ₹20,000 tak ka extra kharch ho sakta hai.
