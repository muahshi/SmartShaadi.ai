3. Day vs Night: Light is Money

Shaadi din mein hai ya raat mein, isse aapka photography bill ₹30,000 tak up-down ho sakta hai.

Day Wedding (Sunlight)

  • Equipment: Fewer external lights needed
  • Vibe: Natural skin tones, Airy look
  • Saving: Approx 15% lower than night rates

Night Wedding (Artificial)

  • Equipment: High ISO cameras, Strobes
  • Vibe: Dramatic, High Contrast, Luxury feel
  • Expense: Extra lighting staff & power backups
