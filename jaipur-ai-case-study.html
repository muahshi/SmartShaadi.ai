Case Study: Jaipur AI Wedding — Kaise Aditi & Rahul Ne ₹3 Lakh Bachaye

📅 2026 ⏱️ 10 min read ✍️ Smart Shaadi AI ← Saare Articles

Yeh ek real-world example hai ki AI wedding planning tools kaise actual paise bachate hain. Aditi Sharma aur Rahul Verma — ek Bangalore couple — ne December 2025 mein Jaipur mein apni destination wedding plan ki. Unke experience se yeh guide banai gayi hai.

💡 Result: Total savings of ₹2.8-3.2 lakh on a ₹35 lakh Jaipur destination wedding budget — approximately 8-9% savings through smart planning and AI tools.

Challenge: Bangalore se Jaipur Planning

Aditi aur Rahul ke paas ek problem thi — dono Bangalore mein kaam karte the aur Jaipur mein wedding plan karna tha. Limited time, limited local knowledge, aur bahut vendors se negotiate karna tha — sab online.

Step 1: AI Budget Planning

SmartShaadi AI chatbot se starting point mila. ₹35 lakh budget ke liye Jaipur-specific breakdown mila — venue 32%, catering 26%, decor 16%, photography 11%, misc 15%. Yeh numbers unhone manually research kiye numbers se kaafi different the (unka initial estimate venue ke liye 40% tha jo AI ne correct kiya).

Step 2: Vendor Negotiation Bot

SmartShaadi ka Vendor Negotiation Bot use karke Rahul ne photographer se negotiate kiya. Bot ne exact script diya — "Aapka portfolio bahut achha hai. Hum pre-wedding shoot include nahi karenge — toh package mein kya adjust kar sakte hain?" Result: ₹85,000 ke package se ₹68,000 mein same deliverables mila. Saving: ₹17,000.

Step 3: Off-Season Booking

AI suggestion thi ki February mein Jaipur wedding karein instead of December (peak). February mein Jaipur mein perfect weather hota hai (15-20°C) aur venue rates 25-30% kam hoti hain. Venue cost: December estimate ₹4.5L → February actual: ₹3.2L. Saving: ₹1.3 lakh.

Step 4: Guest List Optimization

AI tool ne suggest kiya ki 180 guests se 150 karne se ₹3,500 per guest (all-in cost) = ₹1.05 lakh saving. Couple ne carefully 30 "courtesy invites" hataaye. Total saving: ₹1.05 lakh.
