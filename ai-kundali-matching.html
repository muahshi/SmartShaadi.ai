Kundali Matching Kya Hota Hai?

    
Kundali Matching, jise Horoscope Matching ya Guna Milan bhi kehte hain, ek ancient Vedic astrology practice hai jisme shaadi se pehle dulha aur dulhan ki janam kundali (birth chart) ki compatibility check ki jaati hai. Yeh practice hazaaron saalon se Indian families mein follow ki ja rahi hai aur aaj bhi bahut families ke liye shaadi ke decision mein important role play karti hai.


    
Vedic astrology mein, ek insaan ki kundali uski birth date, birth time aur birth place se banati hai. Kundali mein planets (grahas) ki position se uske swabhav, health, career aur relationships ke baare mein predict kiya jaata hai. Jab do logon ki kundali milai jaati hai, toh unke beech ke future ke baare mein analysis ki jaati hai.

Mangal Dosha — Kya Hai Aur Kab Cancel Hota Hai

    
Mangal Dosha tab hota hai jab kisi ki kundali mein Mangal (Mars) pehle, chauthe, saatve, aathve ya barahve ghar mein hota hai. Iska effect marriage pe negative maana jaata hai — especially partner ke health aur marriage longevity pe. Lagbhag 40% logon mein Mangal Dosha hota hai.


    
Cancellation conditions: Bahut cases mein Mangal Dosha automatically cancel ho jaata hai — jab dono partners mein Mangal Dosha ho (double Mangal), certain planetary positions ho, ya specific nakshatras mein janam ho. Hamare AI tool yeh sab automatically check karta hai.


    
Nadi Dosha — Sabse Important Dosha

    
Nadi Dosha teen prakar ki Nadis pe based hai — Aadi Nadi, Madhya Nadi, Manta Nadi. Agar dono ki Nadi same ho toh Nadi Dosha maana jaata hai, jo children ki health pe negative effect karta hai. Guna Milan mein yeh sabse zyada points (8) wala parameter hai. SmartShaadi AI tool Nadi Dosha aur iske cancellation conditions dono check karta hai.
