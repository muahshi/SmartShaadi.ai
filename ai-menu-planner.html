🍽️ Caterer Se Deal Karne Ke 6 Golden Rules

  • ⚖️
    Exact quantities lekar jaao — "200 log hain" se behtar "200 guests, 75kg chawal, 40kg dal, 15kg paneer" — caterer extra charge nahi kar sakta.
  • 📋
    3 caterers se quotes lo — hamesha — 3 quotes lo, compare karo, phir negotiate. Average mein 15-20% difference hota hai.
  • 🥘
    Kam dishes, premium quality — 14 average se behtar 9 excellent dishes. Live counters se perceived value 2x ho jaati hai.
  • 📅
    Off-season mein 20-30% sasta — Same caterer, same food — April-September mein 20-30% less. Smart families off-season book karti hain.
  • 📝
    Written agreement zaroor lo — Menu, quantity, timing, setup, cleanup — sab likhit mein. Yahan Caterer Brief copy karo, 3 caterers ko bhejo.
  • 💰
    Advance 20%, rest on delivery — 50%+ advance mat do. 20% advance, 60% delivery pe, 20% sab theek hone ke baad.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawaal

2026 city-wise: Delhi/Mumbai ₹800-2500, Jaipur/Lucknow ₹500-1500, Tier-2 ₹400-1000. Budget ₹400-700, Mid ₹700-1200, Premium ₹1200-2000. Peak season (Nov-Feb) 10-15% zyada. Live counters ₹100-200 per plate extra.
500 guests (full dinner buffet, North Indian): Chawal 100 kg, Dal 60 kg, Sabzi 90 kg, Maida 75 kg, Paneer 22 kg, Sweets 40 kg, Raita 50L, Welcome Drink 125L. 15% buffer ke saath order karo!
Haan! Live counters ke benefits: Fresh food, entertainment, premium feel — ₹600 ki shaadi ₹1500 wali lagti hai. Chaat counter + Paan counter minimum rakhein. ₹100-150 per person extra mein worth it hai.
Common scams: (1) Quantity kam dena — calculator se verify karo, (2) Cheap ingredients — taste test pehle, (3) Hidden overtime charges — contract mein timing, (4) Staff extra fees — setup/cleanup included karo. Solution: Written contract + exact quantity list.
Tips: Paneer multiple ways (tikka/makhni/kadai), Mushroom + baby corn, Live chaat counter, International section (pasta/pizza), 6-7 sweet varieties, Mocktail bar. Gujarati/Rajasthani cuisine naturally veg aur delicious!
