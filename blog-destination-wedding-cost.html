Destination Wedding Cost 2026 — Goa aur Udaipur Ka Honest Breakdown

📅 2026 ⏱️ 11 min read ✍️ Smart Shaadi AI ← Saare Articles

Destination wedding — sunne mein bohot glamorous lagta hai lekin actually plan karna complex hai. Is guide mein Goa aur Udaipur ka side-by-side honest cost comparison hai — koi hidden charge nahi chhoda.

💡 Destination Wedding vs Local Wedding: Typically 40-80% zyada expensive hota hai per-person cost. Lekin guest count 50-30% kam hota hai toh total comparable ho sakta hai.

✦ Real Couple Story — Destination Wedding

Ishaan & Preethi — Goa, February 2026

NRI couple — Ishaan London se, Preethi Bangalore se. 80 guests, Goa beach wedding. SmartShaadi Destination Calculator use kiya — pata chala February mein Goa peak hai aur 25% premium. March mein same venue ₹40,000 sasta tha. Shifted to early March — ₹40K venue saving, better weather, less tourist crowd. Total kharch: ₹24 lakh all-inclusive. "Calculator ne exactly bataya kaunse mahine mein kitna extra pay karenge — yeh information online kahin nahi milti," Ishaan kehte hain.

Udaipur Wedding Cost Breakdown 2026

Udaipur mein flight + accommodation extra lagi hoti hai Goa se (North India se Goa closer hai). Venue typically ₹2-5L more. But photography outputs are 40% more premium. For the right couple — photography-focused, romantic experience seekers — Udaipur is worth the premium.

Guest Accommodation — Biggest Hidden Cost

Destination wedding mein guests ki accommodation host nahi kar sakte toh expectation set karo clearly. Options: Resort block booking (10+ rooms: 15-20% group discount), nearby budget hotels for family, AirBnB/villa rentals for bridal party. This alone adds ₹5-15L if you're hosting.
